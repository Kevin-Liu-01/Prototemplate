The Transcreate videos play on `/transcreate`, which holds the grid of all
eight, and on `/transcreate/videos`, which holds each video side by side or
stacked. Each original video belongs to its company: Notion, Linear, Ramp,
Asana, Stripe or Miro. `/transcreate/credits` names each original, with its
title and address where they are recorded, and each clip shows 29 to 30
seconds of its original.

The reel, the blog films and the globes are shown live in `/brand` under
Made with the system. The blog films, the mark sting, the release board,
the six translation series films, the Slash announcement with its German,
Spanish and Japanese cuts, How General Translation works and the 24
product films also play on `/motion` in their roster rows, and each
translation series film plays at the head of its package page,
`/motion/<slug>`. A product film's player offers its English captions. Each blog film on `/brand`
and each translation series film on its package page has a link that
downloads its MP4, and each globe has links that download its PNG on its
ground (ink or paper) and its transparent PNG.

The stills `/brand` shows are lossless WebP, written from the PNG with
`cwebp -lossless -z 9 -exact -metadata none <name>.png -o <name>.webp`.
Each WebP is 2 to 38 percent of its PNG's bytes and holds the same pixels.
