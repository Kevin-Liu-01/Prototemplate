# Media made with the system

Finished artwork produced with this toolchain and the Glyphfield studio,
kept here as proof of what the identity does off the page.

| file | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| `open-source-reel.mp4` | the GT Open Source announcement reel: twelve shader materials cut at 0.2s each in the brand blue, landing on the paper gem smoke composition with the $15,000 reveal. Rendered frame-by-frame through the studio's own material engines and Bayer effect layer, scored to the cut grid. |
| `gt-banner-signin.png` | the X profile banner: the sign-in page's halftone dither globe and glyph rain on the left, the customer logo grid on the right, in the 1-bit language. `@2x` is the retina master. |
| `banner-contact-sheet.png` | the process artifact: the ten banner explorations that led to the shipped sign-in globe design, as one contact sheet. |
| `fuma-nama-film.mp4` | the trailer for Taylor Fang's post "Fuma Nama: The philosophy of an open-sourcerer": a HyperFrames composition in the fire gem smoke (`#fe5b16`, `#f7ff61` and white on ink `#070707`), 1920x1080 at 60 fps, H.264 with the moov atom at the front so it plays before it has finished loading. |
| `fuma-nama-poster.jpg` | the Fuma Nama film's poster, its title card, 1920x1080. |
| `designing-docs-film.mp4` | the trailer for Kevin Liu and Taylor Fang's post "Designing docs for humans": a HyperFrames composition in the blue gem smoke (blue smoke on navy, then white and `#86a8ff` smoke on `#2f5ce0`) and the post's blue dither, 1920x1080 at 60 fps, H.264 with the moov atom at the front. |
| `designing-docs-poster.jpg` | the Designing docs film's poster, 1920x1080. |
| `jihe-yuanben-film.mp4` | the translation series film "Ricci, Xu Guangqi, and the Chinese vocabulary of Euclid": how Matteo Ricci and Xu Guangqi put Euclid into Chinese in Beijing in 1606 and 1607, told over scans of an early 17th-century printing of the book, Clavius's Latin Euclid and the Nine Chapters. Made by the Videos session from the research package at `/motion/jihe-yuanben`. 100 s, 1920x1080 at 60 fps, H.264 High at about 3 Mb/s with AAC audio and the moov atom at the front: the Videos session's own web copy of the final, encoded from the 153 MB render. Two Nine Chapters pages are adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0; the end card and `/motion/jihe-yuanben.credits.txt` carry every credit. |
| `jihe-yuanben-poster.jpg` | the Jihe yuanben film's poster, frame 2274 (37.90 s): the book's credit lines, 泰西利瑪竇口譯 and 吳淞徐光啓筆受, with 口譯 and 筆受 ringed in red, 1920x1080. |
| `journey-to-the-west-film.mp4` | the translation series film "Waley, Hu Shih, and the English names of Journey to the West": what Arthur Waley's *Monkey* (1942) and the other English versions kept of each name in 西遊記, told over the 1592 Shidetang woodblock and later prints. Made by the Videos session from the research package at `/motion/journey-to-the-west`. 100 s, 1920x1080 at 60 fps, H.264 High and AAC, the final's streams unchanged with the moov atom at the front. |
| `journey-to-the-west-poster.jpg` | the Journey to the West film's poster, frame 4371 (72.85 s): 弼馬溫 over 避馬瘟, one reading, "ward off horse plague", 1920x1080. |
| `modern-hebrew-film.mp4` | the translation series film "Ben-Yehuda, Pines, and the vocabulary of Modern Hebrew": how Eliezer Ben-Yehuda and Yehiel Michel Pines made new Hebrew words, read through the first volume of Ben-Yehuda's dictionary (1908), from Jaffa in 1881 to the Academy of the Hebrew Language in 1953. Made by the Videos session from the research package at `/motion/modern-hebrew`. 100 s, 1920x1080 at 60 fps, H.264 High and AAC, the final's streams unchanged with the moov atom at the front. The Jaffa meeting poster in it (dated 1913 by its collection) is CC BY-SA 3.0, and the film credits it on screen. |
| `modern-hebrew-poster.jpg` | the Modern Hebrew film's poster, frame 3180 (53.0 s): the bicycle card, *ofnayim* (bicycle) set on the pattern of *oznayim* (ears) as the 1908 volume points them, 1920x1080. |
| `gt-globe-dithered.png` | the dithered globe, a graphic for a partnership: the dashboard sign-in globe (`globe()` in `src/lib/dither.ts`) printed through the 8x8 Bayer screen in `#86a8ff` on ink `#070707`, 6 px cells, 2048x2048. |
| `gt-globe-dithered-mark.png` | the same globe with the doubled-line GT mark at the center in `#f2f2f0` over a knocked-out halo, 2048x2048 on ink. |
| `gt-globe-glyphs.png` | the glyph globe: the same sphere printed in characters from twenty writing systems, land large in `#f2f2f0` and `#86a8ff`, ocean at half size in `#2f5ce0`, the size of each glyph setting its brightness, 2048x2048 on ink. |
| `gt-globe-glyphs-light.png` | the glyph globe in light, 2048x2048 on paper `#ffffff`: land in ink `#070707`, ocean in `#2f5ce0` thinning to `#86a8ff` in the highlight, the halftone run the other way so ink carries the shadow. |
| `gt-globe-*-transparent.png` | each globe's twin: the same art on an alpha ground, for laying beside a partner's mark. A dark twin is for a dark ground and a light twin for a light one. |

The reel, the blog films and the globes are shown live in `/brand` under
Made with the system. The blog films and the three translation series
films also play on `/motion` in their roster rows, and each translation
series film plays at the head of its package page, `/motion/<slug>`. Each
blog film on `/brand` and each translation series film on its package page
has a link that downloads its MP4, and each globe has links that download
its PNG on its ground (ink or paper) and its transparent PNG.
