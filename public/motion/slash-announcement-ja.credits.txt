slash-announcement-ja: credits and sources
General Translation and Slash: the partnership announcement, on Kevin Liu's script, in Japanese.
Film: 38.433 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-10 (LINEUP.md film 27) from the approved
English v8 (films/slash-announcement, 36.283 s, md5 17d9afa94c95230f8b13a65b4ecb017a): the same
picture, motion, bed and order of events, with its narration and on-screen words in Japanese
and every key action retimed to the Japanese takes. The English film is unchanged.

SCRIPT
- Kevin Liu's five voiceover lines as the English film speaks them (its BRIEF.md: lines 1, 3,
  6, 7 and the first sentence of line 8, "Your product should exist in every language."),
  translated into Japanese for this film. The translations were drafted and checked in two
  passes (a draft against the English, then an independent back-translation review) by Claude
  (Anthropic) working in the Videos session on 2026-10-10. They are not General Translation's
  product or API output, and no native speaker has checked them yet: NOTES.md lists every line
  for that check.

SLASH CARD IMAGE (line 1's first sentence)
- Slash card image: Slash. Slash's photo of its brushed-gold Visa Business card on a warm gold
  ground (assets/reference/slash-card-kevin.png, 1080 x 360), supplied by Kevin Liu on
  2026-10-08. The Visa mark on the card is part of Slash's photo. The film shows the photo
  itself, not redrawn and not printed through the screen: lib/card-prep.py cuts the card out
  along its own edges and corners at 16/9 of the photo (Lanczos, a light unsharp mask) and
  extends the photo's own ground to the frame (assets/opener/card.png and ground.png); the film
  turns the card in perspective and sweeps a soft highlight across it (v6: at 0.84 of that
  size, resampled from the photo, so Slash's wordmark fits over it), then (v8) the card slides
  right behind the globe, a little smaller as it goes, resampled from the photo at its size on
  screen in its own canvas (v7 resampled it per pixel into a shape that became the globe; that
  morph is gone). The photo's ground leaves through the film's 3 px screen; the photo itself is
  never printed through the screen.

LOGOS AND MARKS (each drawn from its own file, true: no redrawing, no distortion)
- Slash wordmark: assets/logos/slash-logo.svg, as the GT site serves it (gt-cloud main,
  apps/landing/public/logos/slash-logo.svg). White as supplied everywhere: in the opener (v7,
  on Kevin's note; v6 filled it with the film's olive), in the lockup and on the end card. In
  the opener it is drawn as a vector at its size, with no outline and no shadow; on the gold
  photo behind it it measures about 2.9:1 (2.7:1 at the brightest).
- General Translation doubled-line GT mark: kit/brand/gt-mark.svg (currentColor, filled white).
- The lockup's "x" is Inter 500 (below), set as Kevin Liu's guidance image sets it.

THE LIGHT AND THE SCREEN
- Kevin Liu's guidance image, assets/reference/slash-x-gt-kevin.webp: the palette (olive
  #3b382b, gold #c1a56d, straw #c7b78d, screen gold #c6b75d, white) and the shafts were
  measured from it. The film's light is an analytic field fitted to the image
  (lib/light-fit.json), not the image itself.
- Bayer screen: kit/dither.js (the 8 by 8 Bayer tile), 3 px cells anchored at the frame's top
  left, as every GT film uses (Kevin: "we use 3 px dither"). The 6 px screen v1 measured from
  the guidance image is retired.

THE GLOBE (the approved 19.5 s cut's, ../slash-partnership/lib/film.js, drawn as it draws it)
- Kevin's dithered partnership globe: the sign-in globe of Prototemplate's dither library
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/dither-lib.js, globe(), at the dashboard's sign-in settings:
  ambient 0.14, rim 0.16, landmass 0.42, gamma 1.15, tilt 0.15), redrawn live in the film's
  inks with spin 0.05 rad a second. Its land is procedural noise and claims no real geography;
  the 24 routes from one origin (the approved cut's paths and clock; since v8 2.5 px white
  lines with 2 px white crosses, over a 1 px keyline of the film's olive at 60 percent on each
  side, so they read on the bright land; v4 to v7 drew them 1 px, as the approved cut does) are
  an illustration of the post's "180+ countries" (the figure counts to 180; the routes do not
  stand for countries).
- The glyph globe's twenty writing systems are the partnership still's own glyph list
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/index.html).
- v1's Blue Marble globe (NASA, Reto Stöckli, 2007) is no longer in the film.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), self-hosted by kit/tokens.css

- Canvas text (the lockup's x, the glyph globe): lib/fonts/InterCanvas.woff2, the kit's Inter
  with its cv11 substitution baked in, registered through the FontFace API.
- Japanese: Noto Sans JP 2.004 (variable), Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe, with Reserved Font Name
  'Source', SIL Open Font License 1.1 (fonts/OFL-NotoSansJP.txt), subset to the film's
  characters (fonts/SAJP-NotoSansJP-subset-VF.woff2, fonts/make-subset.sh) and used under the
  private family names 'SA JP' and 'GTCanvasJP', after Inter ('SA Latin', a byte copy of
  kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2) for the Latin in a Japanese line.
- The other scripts on the plates and the page use the machine's fallback faces (PingFang SC,
  Apple SD Gothic Neo, Geeza Pro, Kohinoor Devanagari), as the English film does, and the glyph
  globe's other scripts the system faces.

VOICE
- Narrator: Otani (ElevenLabs library voice 3JDquces8E8bkmvbh6Bc, "Otani - Inviting, Clear and
  Measured", Japanese, middle aged, standard accent, narration), eleven_multilingual_v2 at
  Frederick Surrey's stability 0.55 and style 0.2, speed 1.15 (lines 1, 7 and 8, from
  audio/voice.json, also kept as audio/voice-115.json) and 1.2 (lines 3 and 6, from
  audio/voice-120.json, copied in as audio/voice.json for those two takes). Cast by two rounds
  of auditions against Ryu, Riku, Hinata and Hiroki (NOTES.md).
- Five takes, one per line, recorded on 2026-10-10 with kit/audio/el.mjs line through
  lib/take.sh (--prev and --next set to the neighbouring lines); the voice text writes a few
  words in kana so the voice reads them right. Each take was heard word for word with el.mjs
  hear. No take is slowed or time-stretched.

MUSIC
- The film's composed bed from the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1, instrumental, 75 s requested
  for v1), from the approved Slash film's prompt: "Warm, confident, unhurried instrumental for
  a company announcement. Felt piano and warm electric piano chords over soft upright bass and
  a slow, steady brushed pulse around 80 BPM, major key, spacious. No vocals, no drops, no
  risers, no build. Resolves on a held chord in the last five seconds." This film re-cuts it on
  its own bar lines (from source 1.146 s to the bar line at 12.042 s, then from the bar line at
  36.026 s, the B section, through A' to the held chord), so its held chord lands on the end
  card (lib/splice.py, lib/mix.py).
