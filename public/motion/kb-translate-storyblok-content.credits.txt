kb-translate-storyblok-content: credits and sources
How do I translate content in Storyblok? Film 18 of General Translation's knowledge base series.
There is no knowledge base article for this question yet; the end card points to the docs page,
generaltranslation.com/docs/integrations/storyblok/quickstart.
Film: the product cut, 49.88 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-13. It replaces the story cut
(49.38 s, 2026-10-12), which replaced the built recited cut (v1, 42.50 s, 2026-10-10), never
rendered.

SCRIPT
- From General Translation's docs page "Quickstart" for the Storyblok integration
  (generaltranslation/content docs/en-US/integrations/storyblok/quickstart.mdx, main 261320d),
  with the guides "Configuring Storyblok", "Translating content" and "Using translated content".
  The film tells the quickstart's steps as a story: in Marseille, a French reader sees an English
  headline on a bakery's site, because the Storyblok story holds one English headline and the
  space has no other language. The question card carries the product's name, Storyblok, and its
  answer names General Translation: "General Translation lets you connect your space and translate
  stories with Sync and apply." The steps are the quickstart's: the target languages added to the
  space in Storyblok, the space connected in the General Translation Dashboard with its ID, its
  region and a personal access token, stories and target locales chosen with Auto-enable supported
  fields checked and Sync and apply clicked, the drafts reviewed and published in Storyblok, and
  the story requested from Storyblok's Content Delivery API with the language parameter. Marseille
  is a sample place, and the bakery is the film's sample; the film says nothing about anyone
  there. The Dashboard's labels and what the integration writes back are checked against General
  Translation's own code (gt-cloud main 0844414); Storyblok's menu names against Storyblok's own
  documentation (storyblok.com/docs, read 2026-10-10). BRIEF.md cites each one; the script is
  motion/concepts/kb-story/scripts/kb-translate-storyblok-content.md.

SCREENS
- The Dashboard's All Integrations page, Connect Storyblok dialog and the integration's controls
  are drawn from the Dashboard's code; their figures and other copy are drawn as bars.
- Storyblok's settings and editor are drawn plainly, in bars and boxes, with readable text only for
  the labels the steps need. No Storyblok logo appears: where the Dashboard shows it, the film
  draws a grey square.
- The space ID typed in the dialog, 123456, is an illustrative number. No token is shown; the
  token field is drawn as dots, and the delivery request uses the docs' placeholder,
  YOUR_DELIVERY_TOKEN.
- The site's home page is drawn plainly in a browser: the tab, the address, the site's name and
  the copy as bars, the headline as text. The language code over its corner (fr) is the language
  the reader's browser asks for.

THE STORY'S CONTENT
- The sample story's English headline, "Fresh bread every morning", is invented for the film. The
  Spanish, Japanese and French headlines are drafts written for this film; they are not output of
  General Translation's API, and they wait for a native speaker's review. kb.json holds every one
  of them.

MARKS
- The doubled-line GT mark (kit/brand/gt-mark.svg) on the question card, and the General
  Translation lockup, the bar monogram over the name (kit/marks/bar-monogram-lockup.svg), on the
  end card.

ICONS
- Heroicons 2.2.0 16 solid chevron-down (MIT, Tailwind Labs); Lucide check, plus and cloud-upload
  outlines (ISC), the icon set the Dashboard uses, redrawn with square line ends.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2, through var(--font).
- Geist Mono 1.701 (vercel/geist-font, SIL Open Font License 1.1, kit/kb/fonts/OFL.txt) for code.
- Japanese is set in the machine's Hiragino Sans, named by the kit for text with lang="ja"
  (kit/kb/kb-story.js, langFaces; a system face, fetched from nowhere).

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey, an ElevenLabs library voice (eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55,
  style 0.2, speed 1.0; kit/audio/voice.json). Eight takes, one per line, through kit/kb/take.mjs
  and kit/audio/el.mjs: four recorded 2026-10-12 (the review line retaken once, and the first line
  recorded again when its words changed), the answer recorded again 2026-10-13 with the question
  sent before it, and three recorded 2026-10-10 for the built cut and kept, the lines that add the
  languages, connect the space and run Sync and apply. Storyblok is sent to the voice as "Story
  block". None is slowed, sped or stretched.

MUSIC
- The knowledge base series bed: composed for the series by the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1,
  instrumental, 60 s, 2026-10-09; kit/kb/audio/series-bed.prompt.txt), cut to this film on its own
  bar lines by kit/kb/mix.py so its held chord falls on the end card.

DRAWING
- The drawing grammar of films/gif-how-gt-works (vector interface panels, doubled-line connectors,
  black and white with one blue accent), through motion/kit/kb, its story stage,
  kit/kb/kb-story.js, and the kit's product views.
