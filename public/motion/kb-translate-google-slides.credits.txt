kb-translate-google-slides: credits and sources
How do I translate Google Slides? The launch film for Locadex, General Translation's Google
Workspace add-on, and one of its product films (formerly the knowledge base series), for the
add-on's launch post, X, the Marketplace listing's promo video slot and the Google Slides page.
Film: the story cut, 50.3 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-12; built again 2026-10-13 for the
product pages, with an answer that names General Translation; and again that evening, with the
answer alone on its card; and again 2026-10-14, with the key words of each heading and of the answer
in the blue accent. It replaces the recited cuts (v1 and v2, 40.43 s, 2026-10-10).

SCRIPT
- Written for this film from the Locadex listing on the Google Workspace Marketplace
  (workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/locadex/718285487823, read 2026-10-09), the add-on's source
  (generaltranslation/gt-google-workspace at 6f6d865: src/Plugin.js, src/Sidebar.html,
  docs/API_CONTRACT.md) and General Translation's Google Drive docs (generaltranslation/content
  docs/en-US/integrations/google-drive). The film tells the add-on's use as a story: Acme's team in
  Berlin needs its launch deck in German and every slide is in English, because Drive holds only
  the English deck and translated text rarely takes the same space as the English; the question and
  its answer in the middle of the film ("General Translation lets you choose languages in the
  Locadex add-on and click Sync and apply."); Extensions, Locadex, the languages and Sync and
  apply; the copies with the slides' layout kept, next to the original in Drive. Acme is the
  listing's sample company and Berlin a sample place; the film says nothing about anyone there.
  BRIEF.md and the script cite each claim.

THE PRODUCT ON SCREEN
- The Locadex sidebar is the add-on's own: its markup (captured from its own render functions),
  its stylesheet and its paintProgress, from src/Sidebar.html at 6f6d865
  (FSL-1.1-ALv2, General Translation), driven with status rows in the shapes of
  docs/API_CONTRACT.md. Pixel-identical to the add-on at 2x (tools/check-gws.mjs). Its logo is the
  Locadex mark (generaltranslation.com/brand/locadex-light-no-bg.svg).
- The Google Slides editor and the Google Drive folder are drawings of those products
  (after motion/stills/locadex-listing/editor.js). Google Docs, Google Slides and Google Drive are
  trademarks of Google LLC.
- The deck, "Launch plan 2027", is a made-up company's (Acme), from
  motion/stills/locadex-listing/deck.js, in English and its translations. Its translations were
  written for the Marketplace screenshots; they are not output of General Translation's API, and
  they wait for a native speaker's review. lib/deck.js holds every one of them.
- The copies' names follow the integration's rule (gt-cloud
  packages/integrations/src/providers/google-drive/workflows/copyName.ts).
- The language code over the panel's corner (de, ja, zh) is the language the team presents in,
  then the language of the copy on screen.

MARKS
- The doubled-line GT mark (kit/brand/gt-mark.svg), small in the lower left corner of the answer
  card, in the credit line, and larger on the end card under "Locadex by"; the Locadex mark
  (motion/kit/brand/locadex-mark.svg, cropped) in the credit line.

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey, an ElevenLabs library voice (eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55,
  style 0.2, speed 1.0; kit/audio/voice.json). Eight takes, one per line, recorded 2026-10-12
  through kit/kb/take.mjs and kit/audio/el.mjs (the three lines that say Locadex recorded again
  that afternoon), and the answer recorded again 2026-10-13, with the question sent before it so it
  reads as a reply. None is slowed, sped or stretched.

MUSIC

  instrumental, composed once for the series on 2026-10-09), played from one of its bar lines by
  lib/mix.py so its held chord falls on the end card.

DRAWING
