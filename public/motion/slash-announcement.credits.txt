slash-announcement: credits and sources
General Translation and Slash: the partnership announcement, on Kevin Liu's script.
Film: 74.3 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-07; revised the same day after review.

SCRIPT
- The eight voiceover lines are Kevin Liu's (BRIEF.md), recorded word for word and in order.
  Every line matches the announcement post (../slash-partnership/BRIEF.md) and its follow-up.

LOGOS AND MARKS (each drawn from its own file, true: no redrawing, no recolouring, no distortion)
- Slash wordmark: assets/logos/slash-logo.svg, as the GT site serves it
  (gt-cloud main, apps/landing/public/logos/slash-logo.svg). White as supplied.
- General Translation doubled-line GT mark: kit/brand/gt-mark.svg (currentColor, filled white).
- The lockup's "x" is Inter 500 (below), set as Kevin Liu's guidance image sets it.

THE LIGHT AND THE SCREEN
- Kevin Liu's guidance image, assets/reference/slash-x-gt-kevin.webp: the palette (olive
  #3b382b, gold #c1a56d, straw #c7b78d, screen gold #c6b75d, white), the shafts and the
  6 px Bayer screen were measured from it. The film's light is an analytic field fitted to
  the image (lib/light-fit.json), not the image itself.
- Bayer screen: kit/dither.js (the 8 by 8 Bayer tile), 6 px cells anchored at (0, 0).

PICTURES
- The Blue Marble (lines 1 and 5): kit/sources/earth.jpg.
  Image: NASA, Reto Stöckli, 2007, public domain
  Printed through the film's screen in its inks (lib/globe-prep.py). The 51 route endpoints
  are seeded points on the photo's visible land, at least 40 px apart, and claim no
  individual countries or corridors; the figure counts to 180 on its own clock and carries
  the post's "180+ countries".
- The glyph globe's twenty writing systems are the partnership still's glyph list
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/index.html), as the approved Slash film uses them.
- The app page (line 8) is the GIF film's page (films/gif-how-gt-works), with its strings:
  Welcome back / Hola de nuevo / Bon retour / おかえりなさい, Get started / Comenzar /
  Commencer / 始める. The chevron is Heroicons 2.2.0 16 solid chevron-down (MIT, Tailwind Labs).
- The plates' rows (line 7) are the brand film's sanctioned lines from MOTION.md, cut at word
  boundaries to the phrase "every product" (the Japanese and Korean without their object
  particles): すべての製品, كل منتج, हर उत्पाद, 모든 제품, 每个产品.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), self-hosted by kit/tokens.css
  (kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2) through var(--font); weights 400 and 500.
- Canvas text (the lockup's x, the glyph globe): lib/fonts/InterCanvas.woff2, the kit's Inter
  with its cv11 substitution baked in, registered through the FontFace API.
- Non-Latin text uses the machine's fallback faces (Hiragino Sans, PingFang SC,
  Apple SD Gothic Neo, Geeza Pro, Kohinoor Devanagari and the system faces for the glyph
  globe's other scripts).

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey (ElevenLabs library voice j9jfwdrw7BRfcR43Qohk,
  eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55, style 0.2, speed 1.0), from kit/audio/voice.json.
  Eight takes, one per line, recorded with kit/audio/el.mjs line through lib/take.sh
  (--prev and --next set to the neighbouring lines), each checked with el.mjs hear. No
  retakes. No take is slowed, sped or time-stretched.

MUSIC
- One bed from the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1, instrumental, 75 s requested), from the
  approved Slash film's prompt: "Warm, confident, unhurried instrumental for a company
  announcement. Felt piano and warm electric piano chords over soft upright bass and a
  slow, steady brushed pulse around 80 BPM, major key, spacious. No vocals, no drops, no
  risers, no build. Resolves on a held chord in the last five seconds." A 10 s test came
  first. The bed resolved at 60.0 s of 75, so lib/mix.py extends it on its own bar lines
  (bars 8 to 11 play twice) and starts it three beats in, so its held chord lands on the
  end card.

THE OFFER (as the GT site states it, ../slash-partnership/BRIEF.md)
- generaltranslation.com/slash. Up to $2,000 in translation credits.
- The end card's legal line is the post's sentence, verbatim: "Translation credits are
  subject to General Translation's review and approval."
