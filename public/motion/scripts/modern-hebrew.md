# Modern Hebrew מִלּוֹן

69.6 s · v2 · Narrator: Frederick Surrey

For about seventeen centuries Hebrew was no one’s mother tongue. Eliezer Ben-Yehuda built new words from its own roots and marked his accepted coinages in his dictionary with a small sign. The tomato word he refused to print is the word Hebrew speakers use today.
