# Jihe yuanben 幾何原本

74.8 s · v2 · Narrator: Frederick Surrey · Reader: Yun

The Chinese word for geometry, 幾何, is often said to copy the sound of “geo.” In the 1607 Chinese Euclid it was the ordinary word for “how much.” Only six books of plane geometry existed in Chinese for 250 years, so the word for “how much” came to mean geometry.
