# Jihe yuanben 幾何原本

100.0 s · 100 s cut · Narrator: Clara · Reader: Yun

In Beijing from 1606, Matteo Ricci translated Euclid by mouth and Xu Guangqi wrote it down in Chinese, and because only their six books of plane geometry circulated for 250 years, the word 幾何, which had meant “how much,” came to mean geometry.
