kb-translate-srt-subtitles: credits and sources
How do I translate SRT subtitles? One of General Translation's product films, for its video page
(/product/video). The end card points to the docs page,
generaltranslation.com/docs/cli/reference/formats/srt-files.
Film: the story cut, 51.33 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-14.

SCRIPT
- From General Translation's docs page "SRT" (generaltranslation/content
  docs/en-US/cli/reference/formats/srt-files.mdx, main 361ec4a8), with the CLI quickstart, the
  configuration reference, the credentials guide and the gt translate reference beside it. The film
  tells it as a story: someone in Santiago who reads Spanish turns on the subtitles of a product
  video and every line is English, because the video has one SRT file and its cues are English. The
  answer on the answer card is the page's opening line with General Translation as its subject:
  "General Translation translates the text in SRT subtitle files and keeps every timestamp." The
  steps are the docs': install gt, list the subtitle files in gt.config.json with a [locale] folder,
  sign in, set the project ID and run npx gt translate; each new file keeps the timing lines and cue
  numbers. Santiago is a sample place; the film says nothing about anyone there. The code, commands
  and file names are checked against the docs and the open-source code (generaltranslation/gt at
  1048f503, gt 2.26.5). BRIEF.md cites each one. The research brief is by research lane R1 of the
  Videos session.

CODE ON SCREEN
- subtitles/en/intro.srt (cue 12 is the video page's cue and timing line, cue 13 the film's own
  English), Terminal (npm install gt --save-dev, npx gt login, npx gt translate), gt.config.json
  (the docs' file exactly), .env.local with the docs' placeholder GT_PROJECT_ID=your-project-id,
  and cue 12 of subtitles/en/intro.srt over cue 12 of subtitles/es/intro.srt. No real project ID or
  key appears.

THE VIDEO PAGE
- The cue and its Spanish and French are the strings of General Translation's own video page
  (gt-cloud apps/landing/src/components/pages/seo/SubtitleStage.tsx); they are drafts written for
  that page, not output of General Translation's API, and they wait for a native speaker's review.
  kb.json holds every one of them. The subtitle menu's labels are each language's own name. The
  video's picture is drawn in bars, with no words. The play glyph is Lucide 0.488.0 play (ISC,
  Lucide contributors); the menu's chevron is Heroicons 2.2.0 16 solid chevron-down (MIT, Tailwind
  Labs).
- The language code over the page's corner (es) is the language the viewer's browser asks for.

MARKS
- The doubled-line GT mark (kit/brand/gt-mark.svg), small in the lower left corner of the answer
  card, and the same mark, larger, on the end card.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2, through var(--font).
- Geist Mono 1.701 (vercel/geist-font, SIL Open Font License 1.1, kit/kb/fonts/OFL.txt) for code.

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey, an ElevenLabs library voice (eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55,
  style 0.2, speed 1.0; kit/audio/voice.json). Seven takes, one per line, through kit/kb/take.mjs
  and kit/audio/el.mjs, recorded 2026-10-14 (line config recorded twice, the second used), the
  answer with the question sent before it so it reads as a reply. None is slowed, sped or stretched.

MUSIC
- The product films' series bed: composed for the series by the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1,
  instrumental, 60 s, 2026-10-09; kit/kb/audio/series-bed.prompt.txt), cut to this film on its own
  bar lines by kit/kb/mix.py so its held chord falls on the end card.

DRAWING
- The drawing grammar of films/gif-how-gt-works (vector interface panels, doubled-line connectors,
  black and white with one blue accent), through motion/kit/kb, its story stage
  (kit/kb/kb-story.js), its screen module (kit/kb/kb-screen.js) and its video module
  (kit/kb/kb-video.js) for the video page and its player.
