mark-sting: credits and sources
General Translation's logo sting: the bar monogram assembles and the lockup's wordmark draws under it.
Film: 6.0 s, 60 fps. mark-sting.mp4 (1920 x 1080), mark-sting-square.mp4 (1080 x 1080, laid out
again for the square frame), mark-sting-alpha.mov (1920 x 1080, paper mark on a transparent ground)
and mark-sting-alpha-dark.mov (the same with the mark in ink, for light footage), both ProRes 4444;
each with sound and as a silent copy. Built 2026-10-09; fix pass 2026-10-10 (the official
wordmark, the band at 30 degrees, the dark alpha render).

THE LOCKUP (General Translation's own; drawn from its source files, true: no redrawing)
- The bar monogram, one of the speed marks Kevin Liu chose on 2026-09-29: Prototemplate's
  scripts/build/speed-marks.mjs and public/marks/bar-monogram.svg (identical to
  motion/kit/marks/bar-monogram.svg). The film rebuilds it from the script's own ten rectangles,
  12 degree skew and 8 unit cut so its parts can move; the settled frame is the script's geometry.
- The wordmark: the official lockup's own artwork, the second path of
  public/marks/bar-monogram-lockup.svg (identical to motion/kit/marks/bar-monogram-lockup.svg),
  GENERAL TRANSLATION outlined by build-speed-marks.mjs from Michroma (Copyright 2011 The Michroma
  Project Authors, https://github.com/googlefonts/Michroma-font, SIL Open Font License 1.1). The
  film splits the path into its 18 letters (lib/wordmark.py, verbatim) and draws them at the
  lockup's own size and place. The film sets no type and loads no font.
- One ink: paper #f2f2f0 on ink #070707; the alpha renders carry the mark only (paper, or ink
  #070707 in the dark render).

THE SCREEN AND THE BAND
- kit/dither.js (the 8 by 8 Bayer tile), 3 px cells anchored at the frame's top left: the bars'
  trails, the letters' print and the specular band.
- The band's 30 degree window follows the site's DitheredMark (gt-cloud
  apps/landing/src/components/landing/shared/DitheredMark.tsx).

SOUND
- Two sound effects from the ElevenLabs sound effects API (the sound generation endpoint,
  through motion/kit/audio/el.mjs), generated 2026-10-09: a quiet whoosh under the speed bars and
  a soft landing under the G and T. Mixed by lib/mix.py (high-passed, faded, placed, -21.0 LUFS
  integrated, true peak -2.0 dBTP). No music, no voice.

TOOLS
- HyperFrames 0.8.106 (composition and render), GSAP 3.15 (kit/gsap.min.js), FFmpeg (mix and checks).
