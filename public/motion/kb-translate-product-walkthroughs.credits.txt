kb-translate-product-walkthroughs: credits and sources
How do I show a product walkthrough in every language? One of General Translation's product films,
for its video page (/product/video). The end card points to the docs page,
generaltranslation.com/docs/integrations/rrweb/quickstart.
Film: the story cut, 47.75 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-14.

SCRIPT
- From General Translation's docs page "Quickstart" under rrweb (generaltranslation/content
  docs/en-US/integrations/rrweb/quickstart.mdx, main 361ec4a8), with its guides and references and
  the React config reference's _tagIds section. The film tells it as a story: someone in Frankfurt
  who reads German plays your product walkthrough and every word is English, because it was recorded
  once in English and a recording replays the text it captured. The answer on the answer card is
  the quickstart's opening with General Translation as its subject: "General Translation lets you
  replay a walkthrough with gt-rrweb in every language." The steps are the quickstart's: install
  gt-rrweb with the rrweb packages and turn on _tagIds, mount GTRecorder with the app's catalog
  loader and record in English with German and Japanese listed, and render GTReplayer with the saved
  bundle. Frankfurt is a sample place; the film says nothing about anyone there. The code, commands
  and file names are checked against the docs and the open-source code (generaltranslation/gt at
  1048f503, gt-rrweb 0.2.0). BRIEF.md cites each one. The research brief is by research lane R1 of
  the Videos session.
- The quickstart was followed once end to end before the film was built: a scratch Next.js app
  with gt-next and gt-rrweb from npm recorded a click in a headless browser, and GTReplayer replayed
  the bundle and switched it to German and Japanese in place (NOTES.md, "The rrweb check"). The
  recorded text node on screen, with its id 43, is that run's.

CODE ON SCREEN
- walkthrough.json (one recorded text node), Terminal (npm install gt-rrweb with the rrweb
  packages), gt.config.json (the CLI quickstart's config with the rrweb quickstart's _tagIds),
  src/RecordingTools.tsx (the quickstart's GTRecorder element), src/RecordButton.tsx (the recording
  guide's snippet exactly) and src/Walkthrough.tsx (the quickstart's component, its one long row
  folded).

THE REPLAYED PAGE
- The Projects page, its strings and its German and Japanese are the film's own drafts, not output
  of General Translation's API, and they wait for a native speaker's review. kb.json holds every
  one of them. The player is drawn in the kit's grammar: its locale control as codes (en, de, ja)
  where the product draws flags, its click as the kit's press where the product draws a ring, and
  its cursor, the arrow of gt-rrweb's player (packages/rrweb/src/replay/styles.ts), without a
  shadow. The play glyph is Lucide 0.488.0 play (ISC, Lucide contributors).
- The language code over the player's corner (de) is the language the reader's browser asks for.

MARKS
- The doubled-line GT mark (kit/brand/gt-mark.svg), small in the lower left corner of the answer
  card, and the same mark, larger, on the end card.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2, through var(--font).
- Geist Mono 1.701 (vercel/geist-font, SIL Open Font License 1.1, kit/kb/fonts/OFL.txt) for code.
- Japanese is set in the machine's Hiragino Sans, which kit/kb/kb-story.js names by the text's
  language; no face is fetched.

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey, an ElevenLabs library voice (eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55,
  style 0.2, speed 1.0; kit/audio/voice.json). Seven takes, one per line, through kit/kb/take.mjs
  and kit/audio/el.mjs, recorded 2026-10-14, the answer with the question sent before it so it
  reads as a reply. None is slowed, sped or stretched.

MUSIC
- The product films' series bed: composed for the series by the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1,
  instrumental, 60 s, 2026-10-09; kit/kb/audio/series-bed.prompt.txt), cut to this film on its own
  bar lines by kit/kb/mix.py so its held chord falls on the end card.

DRAWING
- The drawing grammar of films/gif-how-gt-works (vector interface panels, doubled-line connectors,
  black and white with one blue accent), through motion/kit/kb, its story stage
  (kit/kb/kb-story.js), its screen module (kit/kb/kb-screen.js) and its video module
  (kit/kb/kb-video.js) for the replay player and its cursor.
