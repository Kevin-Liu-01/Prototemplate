kb-translate-lottie-animations: credits and sources
How do I translate a Lottie animation? One of General Translation's product films, for its video
page (/product/video). The end card points to the docs page,
generaltranslation.com/docs/cli/reference/formats/lottie-files.
Film: the story cut, 48.48 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-15.

SCRIPT
- From General Translation's docs page "Lottie" (generaltranslation/content
  docs/en-US/cli/reference/formats/lottie-files.mdx, main 361ec4a8), with the gt stage and gt
  download references, the managing translations guide and the configuration reference beside it.
  The film tells it as a story: someone in Lille who reads French opens an app's onboarding page,
  and the words of its animation are English, because they are text inside the .lottie file and
  only an English file exists. The answer on the answer card names General Translation as its
  subject: "General Translation translates Lottie animations with npx gt stage and npx gt
  download." The steps are the docs': list the animations and the fonts they use in
  gt.config.json, sign in, set the project ID and run npx gt stage, which uploads the fonts first,
  then run npx gt download until the Spanish and French files are saved, and review them. Lille is
  a sample place; the film says nothing about anyone there. The code, commands and file names are
  checked against the docs and the open-source code (generaltranslation/gt at 1048f503, gt 2.26.5).
  BRIEF.md cites each one. The research brief is by research lane R1 of the Videos session.

CODE ON SCREEN
- The text document of animations/en/onboarding.lottie (its t, f and s, from the animation JSON
  inside the bundle), gt.config.json (the docs' lottie and fonts entries), Terminal (npx gt login,
  npx gt stage, npx gt download), .env.local with the docs' placeholder
  GT_PROJECT_ID=your-project-id, and the project's files. No real project ID or key appears.

THE ANIMATION
- The onboarding animation on the reader's screen is the film's own, made for it by
  tools/make-lottie.py: a dotLottie file, 464 x 290 at 30 fps, whose title is glyph outlines drawn
  from Inter Medium. It is played by lottie-web 5.13.0 (Airbnb, MIT License), kit/lottie.min.js.
- Its Spanish and French titles are drafts written for the film, not General Translation's output.
  A native speaker reviews them, and the files of a signed-in run of npx gt stage and npx gt
  download replace them before release.
- The language code over the page's corner is the language of the page's state: fr, then es and
  fr in the language pass.

MARKS
- The doubled-line GT mark (kit/brand/gt-mark.svg), small in the lower left corner of the answer
  card, and the General Translation lockup, the bar monogram over the name
  (kit/marks/bar-monogram-lockup.svg), on the end card.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2, through var(--font).
- The animation's title: Inter Medium, the static cut of Inter 4.1 (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font
  License 1.1; fonts/Inter-Medium.ttf and fonts/OFL.txt), as glyph outlines.
- Geist Mono 1.701 (vercel/geist-font, SIL Open Font License 1.1, kit/kb/fonts/OFL.txt) for code.

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey, an ElevenLabs library voice (eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55,
  style 0.2, speed 1.0; kit/audio/voice.json). Seven takes, one per line, through kit/kb/take.mjs
  and kit/audio/el.mjs, recorded 2026-10-15, the answer with the question sent before it so it
  reads as a reply. None is slowed, sped or stretched.

MUSIC
- The product films' series bed: composed for the series by the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1,
  instrumental, 60 s, 2026-10-09; kit/kb/audio/series-bed.prompt.txt), cut to this film on its own
  bar lines by kit/kb/mix.py so its held chord falls on the end card.

DRAWING
- The drawing grammar of films/gif-how-gt-works (vector interface panels, doubled-line connectors,
  black and white with one blue accent), through motion/kit/kb, its story stage
  (kit/kb/kb-story.js), its screen module (kit/kb/kb-screen.js) and its lottie module
  (kit/kb/kb-lottie.js) for the animation on the page.
