slash-partnership: credits and sources
General Translation and Slash: up to $2,000 in translation credits for Slash customers.
Film: 19.5 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. The 20 s cut, built 2026-10-06 and revised the same day
after review (the 47.5 s cut and its credits are in archive-47s/). Re-rendered 2026-10-08 on
the 3 px screen (the 6 px cut is in archive-6px/).

LOGOS AND MARKS (each drawn from its own file, true: no redrawing, no recolouring, no distortion)
- Slash wordmark: assets/logos/slash-logo.svg, as the GT site serves it
  (gt-cloud main, apps/landing/public/logos/slash-logo.svg). White as supplied.
- General Translation doubled-line GT mark: kit/brand/gt-mark.svg (currentColor, filled white).
- The lockup's "x" is Inter 500 (below), set as Kevin Liu's guidance image sets it.

THE LIGHT AND THE SCREEN
- Kevin Liu's guidance image, assets/reference/slash-x-gt-kevin.webp: the palette (olive
  #3b382b, gold #c1a56d, straw #c7b78d, screen gold #c6b75d, white) and the shafts were
  measured from it. The film's light is an analytic field fitted to the image
  (lib/light-fit.json), not the image itself.
- Bayer screen: kit/dither.js (the 8 by 8 Bayer tile), 3 px cells anchored at (0, 0), the
  films' common screen (Kevin, 2026-10-08); the 6 px screen measured from his image is
  retired (archive-6px/).

THE GLOBE
- Kevin's dithered partnership globe: the sign-in globe of Prototemplate's dither library
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/dither-lib.js, globe(), at the dashboard's sign-in
  settings: ambient 0.14, rim 0.16, landmass 0.42, gamma 1.15, tilt 0.15), redrawn live in
  the film's inks with spin 0.05 rad a second. Its land is procedural noise and claims no
  real geography; the 180 routes are an illustration of the post's "180+ countries".
- The glyph globe's twenty writing systems are the partnership still's own glyph list
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/index.html).

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), self-hosted by kit/tokens.css
  (kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2) through var(--font); weights 400 and 500.
- Canvas text (the moving type's cells, the lockup's x): lib/fonts/InterCanvas.woff2, the
  kit's Inter with its cv11 substitution baked in (copied from
  films/blog-designing-docs/lib/fonts), registered through the FontFace API.
- The glyph globe's non-Latin glyphs use the machine's fallback faces (Hiragino Sans,
  PingFang SC, Apple SD Gothic Neo and the system faces for the other scripts).

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey (ElevenLabs library voice j9jfwdrw7BRfcR43Qohk,
  eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55, style 0.2, speed 1.0), from kit/audio/voice.json.
  Three takes, one per line, recorded with kit/audio/el.mjs line (--prev and --next set to
  the neighbouring lines; the last line with no --next), checked with el.mjs hear. Line 1
  is the 47.5 s cut's take; lines 2 and 3 were recorded for this cut. No take is slowed,
  sped or time-stretched.

MUSIC
- One bed from the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1, instrumental, 47.5 s), from the prompt
  in SCRIPT.md ("Warm, confident, unhurried instrumental for a company announcement. Felt
  piano and warm electric piano chords over soft upright bass and a slow, steady brushed
  pulse around 80 BPM ..."), generated for the 47.5 s cut. No new music was made: lib/mix.py
  re-cuts it on its own bar lines (source 2.25 to 12.215 s, then 30.215 s onward, an 80 ms
  equal-power crossfade ending on the bar line), so its held chord lands at film 15.965 s,
  on the end card, and fades over the film's last 0.6 s.

THE OFFER (as the GT site states it, BRIEF.md)
- generaltranslation.com/slash. "$2,000 in credits for Slash customers."
- The end card's legal line is the post's sentence, verbatim: "Translation credits are
