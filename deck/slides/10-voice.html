Writing style

Copy has the register of a technical specification. The same five rules apply to the site, the docs, the blog, and every social post.

SentenceSentences are short and declarative, and each carries one claim.
EvidenceA number or a mechanism stands where a marketing adjective would.
HedgingNothing is hedged. A claim is made or it is left out.
PunctuationExclamation marks and em dashes are not used.
CaseSentence case is used everywhere, and Title Case only on buttons.
AcceptableTranslations are generated at build time and deploy with the app.
