Fixed points

The identity project works from first principles with two fixed points. Everything else is open. No decision is protected by the effort already spent on it. The current system is evidence of what has been tried and does not constrain what is proposed. A proposal is judged on the same success criteria as the current system, and on nothing else.

Fixed
