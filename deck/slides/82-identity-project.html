The identity project

The project runs from September 1 to November 27 in five stages: research, definition, identity design, visual language, and delivery. Constraints are stated once at the start, along with a test list the work is checked against.

InputsThe inputs are prototemplate.com as the handoff site, the brand book, the questionnaire finalized August 11, and the test list.
DecisionsThe four reviews cover the territory, the identity direction, the system, and the handoff. GT selects one option at each and returns one consolidated feedback document within 48 hours.
