The previous mark

The doubled-line monogram was the mark until the logo replaced it on 8 November 2026. Every stroke of its G and T was two parallel lines, the construction the rest of the system uses. It rendered in one color, black on white or white on black, and shipped only as vector geometry. The seven slides that follow present the speed set, the September 2026 exploration in the race-type register.

Locadex has its own mark under the same rules. It uses one color and appears only where it has a function.
