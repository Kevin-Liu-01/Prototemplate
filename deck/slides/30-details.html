Implementation details

Each of these is minor on its own. Together they determine perceived quality.

The top left of the home page close to full scale: the opaque navigation bar, its single bottom hairline, and the start of the hero.

BlackThere are two dark surfaces: ink (#070707) and raised ink (#101010) for panels on it. No page defines its own black or gray.
Customer logosThe customer row uses each customer's SVG logo, one light and one dark file, in grayscale at 62% opacity and aligned at cap height.
NavigationThe navigation bar is opaque with a single bottom hairline and no backdrop blur.
CodeCode, config, and diffs render on the dark panel in white monospace.
ButtonThe marketing pages use one button component: 38px tall, 44px for the large size and below 720px, 6px radius.
Button labelLabels are Title Case at weight 500, white on a black button in light mode and black on a white button in dark mode.
