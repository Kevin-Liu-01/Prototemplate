The Dossier

The Dossier is the reference implementation. The Dossier direction shows how the brand behaves inside the product. It includes the headline that re-renders the whole sentence in each locale on a shared clock, the translate window, the stack diagram with the Locadex dither highlight, the dithered globe, and the four-color dark section. The other directions document the exploration. The capture follows the deck theme, so in dark mode it shows the same fold after the token remap.
