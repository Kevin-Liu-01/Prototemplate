The line law in chrome

Every rule the shell draws is 1px, drawn once, in one of three roles, and the weights carry meaning. Where two components touch, one of them draws the line. The auditor rebuilds every line from computed CSS and fails the round on a double, a shared seam or a stray border.

In the sketch, the ink lines are the seams with one owner each: the sidebar's right edge, the toolbar's bottom and the panel's left edge.

StructuralThe sheet ring, the toolbar's bottom, the sidebar's right edge and every group header: 18 percent ink.
RowList rows, search results, panel rows, table rows and the progress track: 9 percent ink.
FrameThumbnails, page frames, grid tiles and the hover preview: 62 percent, the darkest line, because it holds a picture.
InkOnly as a state: a pressed button, the active frame, the count while it is edited and a field while it holds focus.
Four defectsDoubled lines from two owners within 4px, missing seams, self-stacks and seams too close in tone to their surface.
