Color

The palette is four base colors and one accent color. Text is set in ink, paper, or titanium. Every hairline is the text color at a lower opacity. The accent appears on small elements such as active states and diagram highlights. It is never a fill for large areas. Depth comes from lines and texture. Shadows are not used.

The opacity steps are the same in both themes and are drawn here in the current theme's ink. The site currently uses three blues: #2f5ce0 in the landing engine, #3b82f6 in the docs, #2563eb on the blog. One should be chosen before the handoff.
