The lockup

The name sits under the monogram in Michroma at one fifth of the monogram's cap height, letter-spaced to the monogram's width. The lockup is used on sign-in plates, on deck openers and anywhere the mark stands alone.

Michroma, Orbitron and Anybody come from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. The build converts them to outlines, so the site and the deck load none of them. The row under the lockup shows the monogram at 96, 48 and 24 px on paper and on ink.
