The docs redesign covers

The post on designing docs for humans opens on one of two cover directions, both without text. The ground is one of 42 dithered gem-smoke frames exported from Glyphfield at 1920 by 1080 and placed at an exact 2x, so the dither stays crisp, under a blue duotone that runs from navy to the brand blue. Each cover also exists at 1200 by 630 for OpenGraph.
