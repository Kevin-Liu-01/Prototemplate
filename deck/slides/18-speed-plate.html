The plate

The G and the T are set in Orbitron Black and cut out of a rounded plate under a 14 degree skew. A hazard stripe, bars 9 units wide on an 18 unit period at 35 degrees, is clipped to the plate's right end.

The letters and the stripes are holes, so the mark is one color and the ground shows through. It is the one direction that brings its own ground, which suits avatars, badges and the CLI banner.

The plate is 320 by 120 units with a 12 unit corner radius, and the letters are set at 90 units. Shown at 920 px wide.
