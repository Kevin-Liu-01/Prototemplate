Diagrams

Every diagram is an inline SVG drawn in one grammar. The rows state the grammar, and the four examples below follow it.

StrokesLines are 1px or 1.5px in ink, the body text tone, or the hairline, with square caps and no rounded joins.
LabelsLabels are 20px in the body text tone or 26px in ink, always horizontal and 12px clear of any line.
MarkersA value on a scale or a step on a flow is marked with an 11px filled square in ink.
ArrowheadsArrowheads are never drawn. Direction comes from reading order, a marker, or a short tick.
ObjectsObjects use one isometric projection at 30 degrees, with faces shaded 4, 9, and 15 percent.
LinksLinks between objects use the doubled connector. Flows, axes, and scales use a single 1px line.
