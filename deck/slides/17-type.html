Typography

Switzer is the display and interface typeface, self-hosted in six weights from 300 to 800. Headings use weight 500 at most. Inter, the variable build with an optical size axis, is the reading typeface for long-form text and documentation. Monospace is used only for code, tokens, and technical labels.

This is the working pair going into the identity project. It is not a final decision. Alternatives remain open if they cover the required scripts.
