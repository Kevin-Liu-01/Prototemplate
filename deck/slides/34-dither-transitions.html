The dither transitions

A dithered field changes state by changing tone on one cell grid. No alpha crossfade, wipe or slide takes part. Both states share the cell size and the phase of the Bayer tile, anchored to one page cell, so the first frame of a transition is the last frame of the state before it, cell for cell.

The globe resolves into the Blue Marble on that grid: one smoothstep over 350 ms mixes the two tones, and the ink moves from the globe's blue to the picture's ink on the same curve. A step between pictures is the same mix over 150 ms, an interrupted mix restarts from the frame on screen, and under reduced motion the field shows the end state.

The picture's curve is solved so the mean tone inside the disc equals the globe's; measured on the dashboard build, the lit ratio stays within one percent across the resolve.
