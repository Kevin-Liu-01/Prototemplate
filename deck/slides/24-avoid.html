Anti-patterns

None of these appears on any GT surface.

Monospace is not the brand typeface. Eyebrow labels are not used as decoration. Smooth scrolling and scroll hijacking are not used. Robot and sparkle iconography is not used to represent AI. Flags are not used except as SVG chips next to locale codes. Iridescent gradients and glassmorphism are not used. Em dashes do not appear in rendered text. Exclamation marks are not used.
