The dithered monogram

The monogram is rasterized to 240 by 59 square cells. Each cell is printed or left empty by the 8 by 8 Bayer screen against a density that is 1 across the left third and falls to 0.18 at the right edge, which prints 5,154 cells. It is the deck's own dither applied to the mark.

Shown at 1200 px wide, so each cell is 5 px. The file is one path of unit squares in a 240 by 59 unit viewBox.
