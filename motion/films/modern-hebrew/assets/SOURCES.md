# Sources: modern-hebrew

Every picture and every quoted text the film shows: where it came from, what it shows, its date and edition, its rights, and the credit the film prints. Rights statements are the ones `BRIEF.md` section 4 (Rights) and section 1 (Image notes) give. The scans were downloaded on 2026-10-02 by the dictionary treatment lane (`motion/concepts/modern-hebrew/dictionary/assets/`) from the URLs below and copied here unchanged. Nothing is retouched: stamps, foxing, show-through and scanner marks are left as scanned.

## Not used, and why

- The 1880 column "שתי מלות חדשות" (*Magid Mishneh*, 1 January 1880): the NLI issue scan, which the NLI title record declares public domain, sits behind a human-verification check and must be pulled by hand. The Academy's JPEG of the column is not used (its host page carries "© כל הזכויות שמורות"). The film sets the column's words in type on cards; the scan can replace card 1 when someone downloads it.
- Vols. 9 to 16 of the dictionary: in copyright. Vols. 6 to 8 (posthumous): not needed.
- `File:BYarticle_sign.png` (a user-made chart, CC BY-SA 2.5): not used. The sign is always the printed one.
