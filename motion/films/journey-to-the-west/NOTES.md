# journey-to-the-west: notes

*Waley, Hu Shih, and the English names of Journey to the West.* The second installment of the Chinese series, after jihe-yuanben. Built 2026-10-03 by the builder lane from `SCRIPT.md` (words, times, sources), `CONCEPT.md` (look, type, moves) and `sound/manifest.json` (every clip and the mix). `BRIEF.md` is the source of every fact. Two critic passes followed (`CRITIQUE-1.md`, `CRITIQUE-2.md`) and the final lane rendered a delivery the same day. Three critics then reviewed that delivery (picture, sound and story, facts), and the finish lane fixed what they found and rendered the delivery again ("Critic pass 3" and "The final" below). The winning treatment is The names (`motion/concepts/journey-to-the-west/names/`), chosen over the woodblock and editions treatments.

**Since 2026-10-05 the film is the v2 build** (73.0 s, `SCRIPT-v2.md`, narrator Frederick Surrey): see "v2 build (2026-10-05)" and its "Fix round" at the end of this file. The sections between describe the 100 s cut, whose files are in `archive-100s/` and `sound/takes/archive-100s/`.

## What the film is

A name in this novel is made out of the parts of a character, and every English version decides which parts to keep. The film shows that decision one name at a time, in one ink on one paper. Every name enters as print (the 1592 Shidetang woodblock, the 1626 Huai'an gazetteer, Richard's plates of 1913): a hairline box draws round the printed name, the film's type lands on it at the print's size, and the scan falls away. From then on the type does what the novel or a translator did. Tint (`#bbbbbb`, the grey of the NCL seal) is the one colour for what is set aside. There is no accent colour; the NCL red line is the only colour on screen that is not the film's own.

## How it is built

- `index.html` (written by `tools/compose.py`): loads the faces, the kit's GSAP with MorphSVG and DrawSVG, the data and the builders; mounts the eight beats as sub-compositions `compositions/b1.html` to `b8.html`; places the sound. Its own timeline is empty.
- `compositions/bN.html`: the beat's plates as static `<img>` (so the renderer waits for them) and one call, `JW.mount(id, start, duration)`.
- `lib/jw.js`: the machinery (palette, glyph outlines and component splits, the two-move component travel, registration on print, plates, hairline boxes, typed Old Standard text, credits). `JW.swing` (finish lane) moves a two-character word as one word on one proxy while it turns from a vertical print line into a horizontal row. `lib/b1.js` to `lib/b8.js`: one builder per beat, written in film seconds, so every time in them can be read against `SCRIPT.md`. `lib/film.js`: waits for the faces, builds the beat on a paused timeline shifted to the beat's start, and registers it.
- `data/glyphs.js` (`tools/glyphs.py`): outline contours of the 23 characters drawn as paths (weight 600 for the surname and the stable title, 500 for registered type). `data/plates.js` (`tools/plates.py`): every plate's crop and size. `data/contents.js` (`tools/contents.py`): the 1592 contents grid, with each column's top and bottom border measured inside the column so the veils stop on the printed border (the woodblock lane's veils ran past the page foot).
- `fonts/`: JW Han (Noto Serif CJK TC subset, `make-subset.sh` reads the jihe-yuanben master read only) and JW Latin (Old Standard TT), private family names in `fonts.css`, OFL licences beside them. Pinyin third-tone vowels are written as the base letter plus U+030C (Old Standard has no precomposed ǎ), and typed text is split by grapheme so a mark never leaves its letter.
- `kit` is the shared kit, symlinked read only.

## Determinism

One paused timeline per beat, built after the faces load; no clocks, no randomness, no network. Checked three ways:

1. **Seek order.** In the preview page, every beat's timeline was seeked on a 0.1 s grid forward, backward and in a seeded shuffle, and the computed state of every element (transform, path data, opacity, fill, colour, position, font size, dash) was compared: identical for all eight beats.
2. **Two renders.** Two draft renders (`--workers 3`) match on all 3000 frames by `framemd5`, and their decoded audio is sample-identical. A third render after the last source edit matched the second frame for frame. After critic pass 2, three renders matched on all 3000 frames, but their decoded audio came out as two variants (A, and B = C) differing from 8.7 s by a residual of -45 to -55 dBFS at zero lag (CRITIQUE-2.md, Determinism). The final lane therefore placed one pre-mixed WAV instead of the 24 clips (Sound, below): a mix of one input has nothing to interleave. A replay of the renderer's mix graph on the single file gave sample-identical output in two runs. Then two delivery renders (60 fps, `--workers 3`, the second to a scratch path) matched on all 6000 frames by `framemd5`, their AAC bitstreams were byte-identical, and their decoded audio was sample-identical.
3. **The finish lane (critic pass 3).** Every beat's timeline was seeked on a 0.1 s grid forward, backward and in a seeded shuffle in the preview page, and the computed state of every element (transform, path data, opacity, fill, colour, position, size, dash rounded to 0.01 px) matched in all three orders for all eight beats. Two delivery renders (`--workers 3`, the second to a scratch path) matched on all 6000 frames by `framemd5`; their AAC bitstreams are byte-identical and their decoded audio is identical. `JW.swing` writes both characters of a word from one numeric proxy, as `placeTween` does, and its first frame is the registered placement exactly, so a seek from either side lands on the same values.
4. **Snapshots in two orders.** Frames captured in the same order are pixel-identical. Frames reached by a backward seek (a render worker captures its frames in order) differ by edge antialiasing on 3 of 45 sampled frames in the 弼馬溫 swap (70.7, 71.0, 72.1 s; GPU path-raster history). The element states are identical there.

What it took, for the next editor: (a) every plate is baked at exactly its stage size and drawn one pixel to one pixel at a whole-pixel position (Chrome's choice of image filtering when it downscales was the source of differences between renders); (b) the two camera pushes animate the image's size and offset, not a CSS scale; (c) a moving gloss changes `left`, `top` and `font-size`, never a CSS scale (text in a scaled layer kept a raster scale from earlier frames); (d) placements of path groups are written from a numeric proxy (`JW.placeTween`), because GSAP's string interpolation of a `transform` attribute came back as `scale(0)` after a backward seek, which blanked the 避馬瘟 row.

## Sound

Mixed exactly as `sound/manifest.json` says: 18 narrator clips and 4 reader clips at their manifest starts, the bed `sound/music/bed.wav` and the effects stem `sound/mix/effects.wav` from 0, all at one gain of +0.2 dB (`GAIN` 1.0233 in `tools/compose.py`; +0.5 dB until the finish lane cut the bed to the story), under a true-peak ceiling of -2 dBFS (`tools/premix.py`). `sound/NOTES.md`, "Finish lane", has the bed's sections, the new takes and the de-popping. No clip is moved, trimmed or sped. The bed's ducking and fades are in the file, so no `data-fx-carve` or automation is applied in the composition.

- **The delivery places one file.** Since the final lane, `index.html` holds a single `<audio id="mix">` (track 2, volume 1) playing `sound/mix/film-mix.wav` (48 kHz stereo, 24-bit, 100.000 s), written by `tools/premix.py`: the same sum the renderer made of the 24 clips (22 voice clips, the bed and the effects stem), with each clip at its start rounded to the millisecond as the renderer's `adelay` rounds it. Against a replay of the renderer's own 24-clip mix graph (its s16 prepare step, `amix` and gain, before the AAC encoder) it differs by -89 dB RMS, the 16-bit rounding of the separate clips. The reason is determinism (see Determinism, item 2). To edit the sound in the Studio, set `PREMIX = False` in `tools/compose.py` and re-run it, which places the 24 clips again; after any change to a clip, a cue or the bed, re-run `python3 sound/tools/build_mix.py` (if the bed or effects changed), then `python3 tools/premix.py` and `python3 tools/compose.py`.

- **One change to the sound lane's files:** in `sound/tools/cues.py` the tick stream under the four English titles in beat 6 now uses the composition's row stagger (0.12 s, so "Protector of the Horses" is complete at 74.84 s, before "these") instead of the planned 0.25 s; `sound/tools/build_mix.py` and `sound/tools/manifest.py` were re-run (effects stem and draft mix rebuilt, 165 cues became 154; clips and bed unchanged). Every other cue already sits on the frame its move starts: taps at 1.0, 16.3, 25.1, 28.0, 33.5, 65.6, 81.5, 83.0, 84.0 (16.3 and 65.6 since the finish lane); slides at 34.95, 37.0, 38.5, 39.5, 41.0, 69.5, 70.5, 71.0, 82.0, 84.5; the grain at 8.5; the tick streams at 43.45, 54.5, 57.0, 59.27, 60.11, 60.88, 71.5, 73.5, 89.5, 93.5 and 94.5. - **Measured on the critic pass 1 draft MP4** (`ffmpeg -af ebur128=peak=true`), after critic pass 1 raised every clip by 0.5 dB (`data-volume="1.0593"`, `GAIN` in `tools/compose.py`): -16.0 LUFS integrated, true peak -1.8 dBTP, LRA 3.1 LU; AAC length 100.000 s = video length.

No clip edge, cut, bed entry, bed splice or fade shows a click (the edge transient ratio is at most 1.7, at the silent last sample). Until the finish lane: -16.0 LUFS, -1.8 dBTP, LRA 3.1 LU. Against the draft's audio the final's differs by a residual of -49 dBFS (signal -19 dBFS RMS), the AAC encoder's own noise when its input differs in the last bits; no level, timing or content changed.

## For a later editor

- The opening is sparse by design: from 0.0 to 3.5 s the frame holds only the title column (at full tone, then at a third from 1.0) and the box and type landing on 西遊記; "Monkey" arrives on the narrator's word at 3.45.
- The bed is cut to the story (`sound/tools/build_mix.py`, SECTIONS; `sound/NOTES.md`, "Finish lane"): it enters with the film, rests under beat 2 and beat 6, puts its first full statement on the cut to the surname, and ends on the take's own held A. A change of beat times needs its sections checked against the bar grid (2.857 s).
- 唐三藏 in beat 8 carries `data-layout-allow-occlusion`, and so does the preface line in beat 2: the layout auditor reads the plate row and the print as covering them, and nothing does.
