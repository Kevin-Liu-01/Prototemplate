# journey-to-the-west: critique, pass 1

The critic watched `motion/out/_draft-journey-to-the-west.mp4` as rendered on 2026-10-03 at 03:11 (draft quality, 30 fps, 3000 frames, AAC 48 kHz stereo). The film was judged against MOTION.md's "How a film is judged", BRIEF.md sections 2 to 5, SCRIPT.md, CONCEPT.md and the writing rules.

## How the draft was checked

- **Frames.** 284 frames were extracted at full size: every 0.5 s, and every 0.25 s for 1 s either side of each cut and each major move (8.0, 11.5, 21.0, 24.6, 29.5, 34.95, 47.0, 63.5, 65.0, 69.5, 71.0, 73.0, 78.5, 82.0, 82.5, 84.5, 93.5, 97.35). The frames were read in 3 x 3 sheets, and the moves were read frame by frame at full size: the 孫 open and close (39.4 to 41.9 s), the 弼馬溫 swap (70.9 to 71.6 s), the card re-set (72.9 to 73.6 s) and the 金公 and 木母 travel (84.85 to 85.5 s). Six frames were also read scaled to 1280 x 720 (10.5, 20.0, 29.0, 62.0, 77.0 and 98.5 s).
- **Whole-film scan.** All 3000 frames were decoded at 384 x 216 in grey. The darkest frame has a mean of 224 out of 255, and no frame is blank or black. No frame differs from both of its neighbours while they match each other, so there is no single-frame flicker. The ten largest frame-to-frame changes are the ten cuts in `sound/manifest.json`, each on its planned frame.
- **Sound.** `el.mjs hear` on the draft heard every line as SCRIPT.md writes it, with three differences that are already explained. "dropped" for "drop" (44.4 s) is the recogniser's past tense; `sound/NOTES.md` shows the take has no t release. "a Bima Wen" (64.76 s) is the recogniser's article in front of the reader's 弼馬溫. "Monkey King!" (95.5 s) is punctuation the recogniser added; the take heard alone has none. No voice is tagged in the music.
- **Loudness** (`ffmpeg -af ebur128=peak=true`): -16.5 LUFS integrated, LRA 3.0 LU, true peak -2.3 dBTP. The audio and the video are both 100.000 s. Silence runs from 0.0 to 0.53 s and from 99.49 to 100.0 s, which is the narrator's first breath and the bed's closing fade.
- **Facts.** Every narration line, title card, tag, rendering, gloss and credit was compared with BRIEF.md sections 2 to 5 and the SCRIPT.md audit. Nothing on screen or in the narration contradicts the BRIEF. Every hedge is kept: "dated 1592 by most scholars", "preface dated 壬辰, read as 1592" in every NCL credit, "argued for", "attributed to Wu Cheng'en", "printed 1626", "from 1982" for Jenner with no volume count, and "after an unnamed Chinese illustrated edition" for Richard's plates. No portrait of Wu Cheng'en is shown, and Lovell appears only with the names that item 13 verifies.
- **Chinese.** Every running Chinese line is one text node, vertical where the print is vertical. The registered strings match the print character for character, apart from the print's own glyph variants (所 in the preface, and 溫, whose right-hand top in the faint chapter 4 print cannot be read as 囚 or 日 at this scan's resolution; see "Left as they are", item 6). The pinyin carons (mǎ, bìmǎwēn) and Waley's ê compose correctly at full size and at 1280 x 720.
- **Safe area.** All type sits at least 160 px from the left edge and 120 px from the other three. The nearest is the closing box's right edge at x 1796, which is a hairline and not text. Credits end at or above y 960.

## Left as they are

1. **The chapter list's reading floor (9.0 to 11.5).** The caption is a list of chapter numbers, not a sentence to read word by word. The thirty is said three ways at once: by the narrator, by the counter and by the lit columns. Its floor of 8 s cannot be met without moving the cut at 11.5, which sits on the next line's first word. It now holds 2.5 s.
2. **The sparse openings of beats 1 and 5 (0.0 to 3.5; 47.0 to 54.0).** Both are designed as they are: a single boxed column on white paper, while the narrator speaks over it. NOTES.md records the first, and SCRIPT.md lands the 悟 column on "share". Neither breaks a rule.
3. **"ward off horse plague" before the re-set (72.0 to 72.9).** The gloss is complete only just before the card re-sets. It is not lost: it stands again on the new card's tint line from 73.4 to 78.5, with 避馬瘟 beside it, so its floor is served there.
4. **Small type at 1280 x 720.** The tags (22 px, about 15 px at 720) and credits (21 px, 14 px at 720) read in the 720 frames. CONCEPT.md sets these sizes.
5. **"drop" heard as "dropped" (44.4).** `sound/NOTES.md` has the acoustic check, and either tense is true by the BRIEF. A retake costs credits for no change of meaning.
6. **溫 or 温 in the chapter 4 print.** At the scan's resolution, the right-hand top of the printed character cannot be read as 囚 or 日. If the print writes 温, the registered 溫 differs from the print by a glyph variant, as 所 does in the preface. The film never transcribes the print in running text, and the knockout now covers the print inside the box. Flagged for the native check.
7. **The open items carried in NOTES.md** (the four native-listener approvals and the narrator fallback for 孫) are Kevin's to decide and are not critic defects.
