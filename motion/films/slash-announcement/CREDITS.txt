slash-announcement: credits and sources
General Translation and Slash: the partnership announcement, on Kevin Liu's script.
Film: v8, 36.283 s, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps. Built 2026-10-09 on Kevin's notes on v7 ("its a bit
hard to see the lines", "make the transition less 'weird' ... make it slide right behind the
globe"): from "for" the payment globe grows at its seat in front of Slash's card and the card
slides right behind it (v7's morph of the card into the globe is gone); the globe's 24 routes
and their crosses are drawn wider over a thin olive keyline, so they read on the bright land;
plate 1's light clears with its outline instead of in one frame; and the first plate starts
within 0.5 s of the credits bar landing. The audio (the bed, the five takes and the mix) is
v7's. The v7 build is in archive-v7-build/ and motion/out/v17/.
v7 (2026-10-09, on Kevin's notes on v6): the card itself became the payment globe in one move
from "for"; Slash's wordmark in the opener became the file's own white; the offer's stems and
plates arrived on "localize"; and line 8 became a new take, "Your product should exist in
every language." v6 is in archive-v6/ and motion/out/v16/.
v6 (2026-10-09, on Kevin's note on v5, "i meant the slash logo should be there in the
beginning"): Slash's wordmark stood over the card in the opener, drawn from its file in the
film's olive ink, and left as the card set off for the globe. v5 (with its fix round) is in
archive-v5/ and motion/out/v15/.
v5 (2026-10-08, on Kevin's notes on v4): the hard
cut from Slash's card to the globe is now one move in which the card shrinks into the globe
and becomes the origin of the payment routes, and the "Hello" greetings under the Slash x GT
lockup are gone (the lockup pushes in slowly instead). The v5 fix round (the same day) gives
the card's last step more time and pushes the lockup 9 percent at an even rate. The v5 build
is in archive-v5-build/. v4 (with its fix round) is in archive-v4/ and motion/out/v14/; the v4
build in archive-v4-build/ and motion/out/v13/v4-build/. v3 (the faster cut and its
carry-over) and its credits are in archive-v3-carry/ and motion/out/v13/; v2 (39.98 s) in
archive-v2/ and motion/out/v12/; v1 (74.3 s, eight lines, 2026-10-07) in archive-v1/.

SCRIPT
- Five of Kevin Liu's eight voiceover lines (BRIEF.md), in his order: lines 1, 3, 6, 7 and
  the first sentence of line 8, word for word, except line 3, which follows his note "no
  need to say 'we bank with slash'": "We're excited to partner with Slash to help ambitious
  companies reach customers in every language.", and line 8, which since v7 follows his note
  of 2026-10-09 ("more than one" -> "every"): "Your product should exist in every language."
  Lines 2, 4, 5 and line 8's second sentence were cut to meet his 40 s cap. Every line
  matches the announcement post (../slash-partnership/BRIEF.md) and its follow-up, except
  that line 8 no longer quotes the follow-up post word for word.

SLASH CARD IMAGE (line 1's first sentence)
- Slash card image: Slash. Slash's photo of its brushed-gold Visa Business card on a warm
  gold ground (assets/reference/slash-card-kevin.png, 1080 x 360), supplied by Kevin Liu on
  2026-10-08. The Visa mark on the card is part of Slash's photo. The film shows the photo
  itself, not redrawn and not printed through the screen: lib/card-prep.py cuts the card
  out along its own edges and corners at 16/9 of the photo (Lanczos, a light unsharp mask)
  and extends the photo's own ground to the frame (assets/opener/card.png and ground.png);
  the film turns the card in perspective and sweeps a soft highlight across it (v6: at 0.84
  of that size, resampled from the photo, so Slash's wordmark fits over it), then (v8) the
  card slides right behind the globe, a little smaller as it goes, resampled from the photo
  at its size on screen in its own canvas (v7 resampled it per pixel into a shape that became
  the globe; that morph is gone). The photo's ground leaves through the film's 3 px screen;
  the photo itself is never printed through the screen.

LOGOS AND MARKS (each drawn from its own file, true: no redrawing, no distortion)
- Slash wordmark: assets/logos/slash-logo.svg, as the GT site serves it
  (gt-cloud main, apps/landing/public/logos/slash-logo.svg). White as supplied everywhere:
  in the opener (v7, on Kevin's note; v6 filled it with the film's olive), in the lockup and
  on the end card. In the opener it is drawn as a vector at its size, with no outline and no
  shadow; on the gold photo behind it it measures about 2.9:1 (2.7:1 at the brightest).
- General Translation doubled-line GT mark: kit/brand/gt-mark.svg (currentColor, filled white).
- The lockup's "x" is Inter 500 (below), set as Kevin Liu's guidance image sets it.

THE LIGHT AND THE SCREEN
- Kevin Liu's guidance image, assets/reference/slash-x-gt-kevin.webp: the palette (olive
  #3b382b, gold #c1a56d, straw #c7b78d, screen gold #c6b75d, white) and the shafts were
  measured from it. The film's light is an analytic field fitted to the image
  (lib/light-fit.json), not the image itself.
- Bayer screen: kit/dither.js (the 8 by 8 Bayer tile), 3 px cells anchored at the frame's
  top left, as every GT film uses (Kevin: "we use 3 px dither"). The 6 px screen v1 measured
  from the guidance image is retired.

THE GLOBE (the approved 19.5 s cut's, ../slash-partnership/lib/film.js, drawn as it draws it)
- Kevin's dithered partnership globe: the sign-in globe of Prototemplate's dither library
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/dither-lib.js, globe(), at the dashboard's sign-in
  settings: ambient 0.14, rim 0.16, landmass 0.42, gamma 1.15, tilt 0.15), redrawn live in
  the film's inks with spin 0.05 rad a second. Its land is procedural noise and claims no
  real geography; the 24 routes from one origin (the approved cut's paths and clock; since v8
  2.5 px white lines with 2 px white crosses, over a 1 px keyline of the film's olive at 60
  percent on each side, so they read on the bright land; v4 to v7 drew them 1 px, as the
  approved cut does) are an illustration of the post's
  "180+ countries" (the figure counts to 180; the routes do not stand for countries).
- The glyph globe's twenty writing systems are the partnership still's own glyph list
  (motion/stills/partnership-globe/index.html).
- v1's Blue Marble globe (NASA, Reto Stöckli, 2007) is no longer in the film.

TYPE
- Inter (rsms/inter, SIL Open Font License 1.1), self-hosted by kit/tokens.css
  (kit/fonts/InterVariable.woff2) through var(--font); weights 400 and 500.
- Canvas text (the lockup's x, the glyph globe): lib/fonts/InterCanvas.woff2, the kit's Inter
  with its cv11 substitution baked in, registered through the FontFace API.
- Non-Latin text uses the machine's fallback faces (Hiragino Sans, PingFang SC,
  Apple SD Gothic Neo, Geeza Pro, Kohinoor Devanagari and the system faces for the glyph
  globe's other scripts).

VOICE
- Narrator: Frederick Surrey (ElevenLabs library voice j9jfwdrw7BRfcR43Qohk,
  eleven_multilingual_v2, stability 0.55, style 0.2, speed 1.0), from kit/audio/voice.json.

  "every"), was recorded for v7 on 2026-10-09 in four takes; the third (audio/vo-8-v7-t3.mp3)

  should exist in more than one language." No take is slowed, sped or time-stretched.

MUSIC
- The film's composed bed from the ElevenLabs Music API (music_v1, instrumental, 75 s
  requested for v1), from the approved Slash film's prompt: "Warm, confident, unhurried
  instrumental for a company announcement. Felt piano and warm electric piano chords over
  soft upright bass and a slow, steady brushed pulse around 80 BPM, major key, spacious. No
  vocals, no drops, no risers, no build. Resolves on a held chord in the last five
  seconds." No new music was made for v2 to v8: lib/mix.py re-cuts the bed on its own bar
  lines (v3: from source 0.277 s to the bar line at 9.044 s, then from the bar line at
  36.026 s, the B section, through A' to the held chord), so its held chord lands on the
  end card.
