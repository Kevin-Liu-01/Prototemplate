## Facts behind the script

Every voiceover line matches the announcement post (`../slash-partnership/BRIEF.md`, which holds the post verbatim and the offer as the GT site states it):

- Line 1: "Slash is building the financial command center for businesses." and "Slash supports sending and receiving global payments to 180+ countries".
- Line 2: "recently introduced Slash Global Cards to allow international businesses to spend USD without a U.S. bank account". The post gives no year, so "Introduced in 2026" is not used on screen unless Kevin confirms it.
- Line 3: "We use Slash ourselves, so this is personal."
- Line 4: "We've built the full stack for localization: connecting code, content, translations, and review workflows into one platform."
- Line 5: "We take a similar technical approach to product localization: making it easy and frictionless for companies to reach customers wherever they are and in whatever language they use."
- Lines 6 and 7: "up to $2,000 in translation credits to eligible Slash customers"; "translation credits can be applied to localize your app, website, documentation, slides, design files, and other supported content workflows".
- Line 8: the follow-up post, "your product should exist in more than one language"; the social blurb, "support stellar businesses to scale to every market". From v7 the film says "every language", on Kevin's 2026-10-09 note (above), so this line no longer quotes the post word for word.
- The claim page is generaltranslation.com/slash (it redirects to /enterprise/contact/slash). The post's legal line: "Translation credits are subject to General Translation's review and approval."
