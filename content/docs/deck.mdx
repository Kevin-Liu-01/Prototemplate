The brand deck is General Translation's identity in 93 slides. Its sources live in `deck/` and its built file is `public/brand-deck.html`.

## The sheet and the grammar

- Every slide is one HTML file in `deck/slides/`, drawn on a 1600 by 900 sheet that scales to the window.
- `deck/parts/` holds the head, the tail and the shared styles: the Inter type sizes, the color tokens for both themes and the layout classes.
- Icons come from the Heroicons 20 solid sprite. Mood slides carry dithered artifact pictures ([Artifact pictures](/docs/handbook/artifact-pictures)).
- `deck/DECK-GRAMMAR.md` is the written grammar: what a slide may hold and how a story is told across slides.

## The viewer

The viewer around the sheet follows the one Turboslide draws for the same deck at [turboslide.com/deck/gt-brand](https://www.turboslide.com/deck/gt-brand), the editor the deck grew into.

- The list is 256px wide with thumbnails and 208px in outline density. Its nameplate links to the Prototemplate home at `/`, a filter row narrows the slides by title or number, and each section folds under its head.
- The toolbar holds Previous and Next around the slide counter, the deck's name, the Slide, Grid and Book switch, Surfaces, Theme, a Turboslide link that opens the deck there, and Present. When the bar runs out of room, `tighten()` in `deck/parts/tail.html` drops labels in three steps.

## The build

`pnpm build:deck` (`scripts/build/deck.mjs`) assembles the slides into `public/brand-deck.html` and writes each picture to `public/deck-assets/` under a name made from a hash of its bytes, so a changed picture never shows its old bytes from a cache. The build also writes the deck's head: its title, its description, its canonical (`https://www.prototemplate.com/deck`), `og:url` and its social card.

## Where it is served

- `/deck` is a rewrite to `public/brand-deck.html` in `next.config.ts`. A link to `/deck` loads it as a document (`src/lib/document-routes.ts`), since a router navigation would fetch the whole file once as a page payload and again as a document.
- `/brand-deck.html` answers the same file with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, so only `/deck` is indexed.

## Who changes it

The onboarding and dashboard session owns `deck/` and pushes its changes from its own worktree. Another session that needs a slide changed asks that session by name. The [gt-deck skill](/skills/gt-deck) holds the procedure for adding, editing and checking a slide.
