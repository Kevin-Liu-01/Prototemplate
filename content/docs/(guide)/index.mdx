Prototemplate is Kevin Liu's hub and working wiki for his work at General Translation (GT), the localization platform for developers. It is a Next.js 16 site served at [www.prototemplate.com](https://www.prototemplate.com), the canonical address, with prototemplate.vercel.app and prototemplate.kevinliu.studio as aliases. Two Vercel projects deploy the `main` branch of the public repository, [Kevin-Liu-01/Prototemplate](https://github.com/Kevin-Liu-01/Prototemplate): the General Translation team's project serves www, and Kevin's personal project serves the two aliases.

## The five tabs

The docs sit in five tabs, and the menu at the top of the sidebar switches between them. The Guide card opens the handbook, and every other card opens its tab's first page.

## The order to read it in

An agent reads three things before it works:

1. The [agent guide](/docs/handbook/agents).
2. The [operating principles](/docs/handbook/operating-principles).
3. The skill for its task at [/skills](/skills). The skill's Review checklist is the bar for the work.

A person new to the hub reads in this order:

1. [Get started](/docs/get-started), to run the site.
2. The [handbook](/docs/handbook), for how the work is done.
3. The tab for the work at hand: [Components](/docs/components) to build a surface, [Craft](/docs/craft) for the design numbers, [Engineering](/docs/engineering) for the browser and build traps, and [Internals](/docs/internals) to change the hub itself.

## What it holds

- **The design lab** at `/`: twenty-seven directions for the GT website, each a built page that runs live, with the anatomy wall and the capabilities ledger.
- **The brand book** at `/brand` and the **brand deck** at `/deck`, 93 slides in their own viewer.
- **The handbook** at [/docs/handbook](/docs/handbook), a section of these docs: how Kevin runs GT work, and the canon: the agent guide, `BRAND.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, the ship loop, the tools, the libraries, the graphics pipeline and the artifact pictures.
- **The skills** at [/skills](/skills): the curated agent skills, each installable in any project.
- **The marks**, **the graphics** and **the films** at `/marks`, `/graphics` and `/motion`.
- **These docs** at `/docs`, in the layout generaltranslation.com/docs runs, in five tabs: the Guide with the handbook, the live component library at [/docs/components](/docs/components) with the blog globe figure under Drawings, Craft, Engineering and Internals.

## Who reads it

- **Kevin**, who reviews every change on localhost before it reaches `main`.
- **Agents** working in this repository, and agents in other repositories that installed its skills or copied its handbook.
- **The brand agency**, which reads the brand book, the deck and the design lab.
